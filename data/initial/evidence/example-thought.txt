Title: Initial thoughts on ACME's AI positioning

I've been thinking about ACME's positioning in the AI infrastructure space. They seem to be capturing a disproportionate share of enterprise AI workloads, particularly in the financial services vertical.

Key observations:
- Their GPU utilization rates are consistently above 85%, suggesting strong demand
- Recent partnerships with major banks indicate enterprise traction
- Pricing power seems intact despite competition from hyperscalers

Concerns:
- Supply chain constraints could limit growth in H1 2026
- Competitive pressure from cloud providers offering similar services

This warrants deeper investigation into their Q4 results and forward guidance.
